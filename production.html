Film & music videos

Picture and
sound, together.

Most productions hand the music off at the end. We make both, so the film is shot knowing what it will sound like, and the score is written for what's actually on screen.

  1. 01 Concept and treatment. The idea, the look and the plan to shoot it.
  2. 02 The shoot. Direction, crew and everything on the day.
  3. 03 Score and soundtrack. Written to picture, recorded with live players.
  4. 04 Post. Edit through to the final mix, delivered ready to screen or release.
