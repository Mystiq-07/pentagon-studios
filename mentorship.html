The Programs

Two ways in.

Different structure, different pace — pick what fits where you are.

Studio Class:

A batch-based program built around skill development. You learn, practise, and build — with structured guidance throughout. Limited slots per batch. At the end, you get guidance to create your portfolio.

20 weeks

Online & offline

Residency:

An apprenticeship built entirely around you. No batch, no fixed syllabus — focused work on what you need, with resources curated to match. Includes a 2-week studio internship with Pentagon. At the end, you'll have a song or project produced and released under Pentagon Studios.

10 – 15 months

Serious about music.
